chowdown security policy

Report a vulnerability in https://chowdown.apps.mrida.ng, including its MCP
server at /mcp, by email to mridang.agarwalla@gmail.com. Please include
what you found, the address or request it affects, and the steps to reproduce
it. Do not include other people's data; a description or a screenshot of your
own test account is enough.

What to expect
- An acknowledgement within 5 days.
- A fix, or an honest answer about why there is none, within 90 days. You will
  be told when it is fixed, and credited if you want to be.
- No legal action against anyone who reports in good faith, follows this
  policy, and stops at proving the problem: do not read, change or delete
  anyone else's data, do not degrade the service for others, and give a
  reasonable time to fix before you share the details.

In scope
- https://chowdown.apps.mrida.ng and its API.
- The OAuth sign-in and the MCP connector.

Out of scope
- Google's own services (Sign-In, Drive), Cloudflare's platform, and the
  assistants (ChatGPT, Claude and others) that connect to chowdown.
- Denial of service by volume, spam, social engineering, and physical attacks.
- Missing headers or settings with no demonstrable impact.

This policy is also linked from https://chowdown.apps.mrida.ng/.well-known/security.txt
